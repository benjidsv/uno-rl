No level deviates when pooled (|z| < 1.5), and the three share the held-out

their deviations are correlated. The miss is chance: about 100 on-seeds checks
across the four runs make one |z| just above 3 roughly a one-in-four event. The
threshold stays at 3 per check, chosen before the re-run.

Reading:

1. **On the forced-play toys the fill-in moves the exploitability by 0.1-0.7
   points; on the free-play ones by 1.2-2.9.** An exploiter reaches the unreached
   sets mostly by drawing with a play in hand, which Random never does.
2. **The contrast between forced and free play survives at about half the size.**
   Nash-filled, the best response to Random is 1.1-1.7 points exploitable on the
   forced toys and 2.4-3.4 on the free ones (4.6-6.3 as first reported). "Near-Nash
   against Random" holds on the forced toys; on the free toys the best response to
   Random stays measurably exploitable, not by the full 6 points.

   first-orbit fill-in; filled, the two seats are close (3.06 / 2.44).
4. Nash-filled is one reasonable fill-in, not the least exploitable one; solving the
   unreached sets for minimal exploitability would give lower numbers still, and
