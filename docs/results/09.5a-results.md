## What was built

- `uno/eval/decisions.py`: `node_kind`, `action_class`, `situation` (a partition
  of decisions and their actions, as functions of an observation, not a `State`,
  so they apply to played games and to a compiled tree's information sets alike),
  plus `classify` (moved here unchanged from `uno.experiments.transfer`) and
  `set_labels` (cached per tree via `uno.store.load_or_label`, D2's grouping).
- `CompiledTree.keying_of`, `weights`, `profile_weights` and `best_response` now
  accept an explicit `Keying`, not only a tree's own `Level`, plus a new `own`
  parameter on `best_response` for imperfect-recall keyings.
- `uno/compiled/coarse.py`: K0 / K1 / K2 history-free keys, `coarsen` (a new
  `Keying` over a tree's own nodes/arcs, grouped by a key function), and
  `memoryless_response` (an iterated greedy best response, D3).
- `uno/compiled/edge.py`: the exact D2/D4 decomposition (`mirror_terms`,
  `forward_terms`, `br_gap`), the `stakes` and `wild_split` diagnostics, and the
  sampled cross-check (`sampled_mirror`).
- `uno/compiled/lookup.py`: `NodeIndex`, a played state → compiled-tree node/arc
  lookup via the L2 keying.
- `uno/eval/estimate.py`: `Comparison` gains `identical` / `diverge_at` /
  `diverge_frac` (the divergence statistic), `compare(..., divergence=True)`.
- `uno/experiments/edge.py`, `memory.py`, `deal_model.py` (new); `pairing.py`
  prints the divergence stat and its rank correlation with the variance ratio;
  `robustness.py`'s deal-model section factored into
  `uno.experiments.common.deal_model_check`, shared with `deal_model.py`.
- Docs: `docs/spec.md` sections 20-22.

## 4. The divergence statistic (`compare(..., divergence=True)`, `uv run uno pairing`)

Full production run, 4000 held-out seeds. 1 failed check
(`heuristic_eps05_own` vs random, seat 1, z -3.26), identical to the one
already investigated and judged chance in step 9 (`09-results.md` section 6):
same seeds, same agents, same z to two decimals, nothing new.

**Identical-games share and variance ratio, `heuristic` vs a variant, against
the `random` column (points to seat 0, pile draws):**

| variant | shared stream? | identical | var ratio | deals for 3 pts, 80 % power |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `heuristic_t1` | yes | 0.74 | 0.23 | 1,778 |
| `heuristic_eps05` | yes | 0.77 | 0.20 | 1,534 |
| `heuristic_eps05_own` | no (own stream) | 0.14 | 0.72 | 5,587 |
| `random` | n/a (dissimilar policy) | 0.01 | 0.89 | 7,088 |

**The identical-games share is what the variance ratio actually tracks**:
rank correlation over all 32 (comparison, seat) cells of the full run, -0.956.
This makes the step 9 finding exact rather than qualitative: pairing's gain
against a *fixed* opponent comes entirely from how long the two rows' own
games stay on the identical path under common random numbers, and sharing an
action-rng stream (not just being a similar policy) is what keeps that share
high -- `heuristic_eps05_own` (the same 5 % mixture, but its own stream) sees
its identical share collapse from 0.77 to 0.14, matching its variance ratio
worsening from 0.20 to 0.72, even though the *policy* distance from
`heuristic` is identical to `heuristic_eps05`'s. Among the non-identical
deals, the mean divergence point sits at 0.24-0.29 of the shorter game's
length for the near-identical variants (they diverge early once they diverge
at all) and 0.10-0.15 for `random` (an immediately different policy at the
very first decision most of the time).

Runtime and memory: the divergence check itself does not change the compute
budget appreciably. `full_uno`'s trace bookkeeping (needed for divergence, not
present in step 9's original `pairing`) does matter for memory: see section 6
item 4.

## 6. Bugs found and fixed while building this

Four bugs. The first three were caught by tests before any number above was
reported; the fourth was caught live, by the user watching memory climb during
the `pairing` production run.

1. **`project_onto_coarse` wasn't orbit-uniform on the coarse keying's own
   (joined) orbits** (only on the finer orbits of the representative L1 set it
   was read from). Reproduces the step 8.6 "a pure choice on an orbit scores
   above the true value" bug one level up. Fixed with `Keying.orbit_mean`.
2. **Coarse sets must be numbered contiguously per tree level, not just share
   one level each.** `best_response`'s per-level backward pass assumes a
   level's responder sets form a contiguous id range (true by construction for
   `compile_tree`'s own keyings, "numbered in order of first appearance"); a
   coarse keying sorted by key value alone can interleave sets from different
   levels under the wrong order, silently finalising a set's choice one level
   too early. Passed every small-ruleset unit test and looked
   self-consistent (`best_response`'s own reported value matched its
   internal bookkeeping) on `SMALL`'s non-symmetric tree; caught only by
   comparing an `own`-weighted best response's value against `own=None`'s,
   which must be identical on a perfect-recall keying (a value mismatch there
   is not a borderline finding, it is a bug). Fixed by sorting coarse sets by
   `(tree level, key)`.
3. **`best_response`'s new `own` parameter folded `own` into the `unreached`
   mask, not only into the argmax weighting.** A set `own`'s current policy
   never visits (but chance x opponent alone can still reach) was wrongly
   treated as unreached and given an arbitrary fill-in instead of a real
   choice -- even though the strategy `best_response` is about to choose is
   free to differ from `own` and might well visit it. This directly lowered
   `memoryless_response`'s reported values (a real loss of achievable value,
   not just a display bug): fixing it raised `TOY` K1 seat 0 from 0.5787 to
   0.5856 (closer to BR L1's 0.5868) and K2 seat 1 from 0.0868 to 0.0997
   (closer to 0.1026). Fixed by computing `unreached` from chance x opponent
   reach alone, and falling back to that (not to a degenerate all-zero tie)
   for the argmax weighting wherever a set's own-reach under `own` is zero.
4. **`uno.experiments.pairing.full_uno` held all 20 `(draws, column, row)`
   `play_pairs` results -- each with a full game trace, ~1 GB for a `FULL_UNO`
   set of 4000 games -- simultaneously, before computing any statistic.** This
   predates step 9.5a (the traces were always built and retained, just
   unused by sections 2a/2b, which only read scores); the divergence
   statistic (section 2c) made the retained traces load-bearing rather than
   wasted, and holding on the order of 20 GB crashed a machine with ~17 GB of
   RAM. Caught live: memory stayed flat for minutes, then climbed steadily to
   4.35 GB and was still growing when the process was killed by the OS.
   Fixed by computing each comparison against `"heuristic"` (section 2c) as
   soon as both sides are available and immediately slimming the trace away
   (the same pattern `uno.experiments.transfer`'s `Played.pairs` already
   uses); peak memory now holds at most two full-trace game sets at once.
   Verified to reproduce identical output on a 200-game run before rerunning
   at full scale.

All four are documented in `uno/compiled/coarse.py` / `solvers.py` /
`uno/experiments/pairing.py`'s own docstrings, not only here.
